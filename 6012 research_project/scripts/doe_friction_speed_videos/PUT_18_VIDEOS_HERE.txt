Place the 18 friction-versus-cycle-duration recordings in this folder.

Recommended filename format:
run01_high_cycle1p5s_rep2.mp4

Use "high" or "low", cycle durations "1p5s", "3s" or "6s", and repeats
"rep1", "rep2" or "rep3". The first number is the randomised recording order.

The script creates experiment_manifest.csv. If your existing filenames use a
different format, run once, fill the missing manifest fields, and run again.
